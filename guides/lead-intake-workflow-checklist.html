Lead operations

A simple lead intake workflow for small teams

Lead intake is often a strong automation candidate because the same normalization, qualification, and routing work repeats every day.

Capture the same fields every time

  • Name
  • Company
  • Professional work email
  • Source
  • Stated need
  • Timeframe
  • Missing information
  • Next action

Normalize before you route

Standardize company names, validate required fields, remove obvious duplicates, preserve the original source, and avoid inventing missing facts.

Use simple qualification buckets

High priority can mean a relevant buyer with a clear need and near-term next step. Medium can mean a relevant buyer with incomplete scope. Low can mean there is no clear business need or the contact data is unreliable.

Keep human approval for commitments

Pricing, contracts, access permissions, payments, and sensitive data should not be auto-approved just because the intake process is automated.
