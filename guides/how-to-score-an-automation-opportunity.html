How to score an automation opportunity before you build it

A good automation target is repetitive, rule-driven, valuable, and easy to review. Use a simple score before choosing tools.

FactorAsk yourself
FrequencyHow often does this workflow happen?
Time consumedHow much manual effort does each cycle require?
Rule clarityCan the decision logic be written down?
Data consistencyDo the inputs arrive in predictable formats?
Error costWhat happens when the process goes wrong?
Ease of human reviewCan a person quickly inspect exceptions?
Business valueDoes fixing this materially improve operations?

Use a 1–5 score

Score every factor from 1 to 5. A high score does not automatically mean “automate everything.” It means the workflow deserves a closer look. If rule clarity or data consistency is weak, standardize the manual process first.

Keep approvals where risk is high

Payments, legal commitments, account changes, sensitive customer communication, and credential handling should keep a human approval point unless there is a strong reason and appropriate controls.
