What a signal looks like

EMPLOYERS · Texas drywall contractor appetite Expansion signal

EMPLOYERS currently says it is expanding contractor appetite and publicly lists Texas drywall classifications including 5437 (Sheetrock Installation) and 5474 (Taping, Floating & Texturing).

Source: EMPLOYERS public contractor appetite page · observed Sep. 21, 2026
AmTrust · Texas artisan contractor availability Recheck signal

An October 2025 public guide said certain expanded artisan-contractor classes were not yet approved in Texas. Appetite Delta treats that as a historical baseline requiring current re-verification rather than presenting an old PDF as a current answer.

Source: AmTrust public appetite guide · historical baseline
Chubb · structured commercial appetite Baseline signal

Chubb provides a public commercial appetite tool using revenue, state and industry/SIC/NAICS inputs. Structured public tools like this make it possible to record a baseline and detect later changes.

Source: Chubb public Commercial Risk Appetite Tool · observed Sep. 21, 2026

A signal is not an underwriting decision. It is a dated, source-backed change or baseline that helps an insurance professional know what deserves a fresh look.
