AI meeting assistants: competitive snapshot

A compact example of the kind of decision-ready brief JayTaskEngine can produce. This is not a recommendation or market-share estimate; it is a dated comparison of publicly stated positioning, packaging, and product emphasis.

Executive read

The category is converging on more than transcription. The strongest public positioning now emphasizes workflow completion, team knowledge, CRM integration, and AI over accumulated meeting context. Free tiers remain common, so differentiation increasingly moves into advanced AI, integrations, governance, team analytics, and cross-meeting intelligence.

What appears strategically important

  • “Notes” are becoming table stakes. Vendors are expanding into workflow automation, CRM updates, AI search, and knowledge reuse.
  • Free acquisition remains aggressive. Several vendors keep meaningful free tiers, which makes paid packaging depend on advanced AI, history, admin, governance, integrations, or team intelligence.
  • Privacy and control are becoming positioning levers. Granola emphasizes private-by-default notes; enterprise/team vendors increasingly highlight retention, admin, security and governance.
  • Integration depth is a visible battleground. CRM, Slack, Notion, Zapier and cross-app AI context appear repeatedly in paid-tier positioning.
