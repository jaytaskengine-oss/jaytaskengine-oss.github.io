How JayTaskEngine approaches delivery

Commercial scope and pricing live on the WordPress business site. This repository documents how technical work is structured and reviewed.

1. Bound the problemDefine inputs, expected output, environment, constraints, and acceptance criteria before implementation.
2. Make the change reviewablePrefer focused changes, explicit error handling, tests where they add confidence, and documentation that explains the handoff.
3. Hand back controlDeliver code, notes, and next-step context without creating unnecessary lock-in or pretending a demo is customer proof.
