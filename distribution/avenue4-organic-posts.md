## Short post 1
Most small-business automation projects start too big.

A better first step:
1. pick one repetitive task,
2. write down the trigger,
3. list the inputs,
4. define the output,
5. keep a human review step for exceptions.

## Short post 2
A workflow is a strong automation candidate when it is frequent, rule-driven, predictable, and easy to review.

If the rules are unclear, standardize the manual process before automating it.

## Short post 3
One underrated automation target: lead intake.

Normalize the company name, validate required fields, remove obvious duplicates, preserve the source, then route the lead by a simple qualification rule.

Keep pricing/contracts behind human approval.

Guide:
https://jaytaskengine-oss.github.io/guides/lead-intake-workflow-checklist.html?utm_source=social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=automation_pack

## Short post 4
If the same task appears every week under “what consumed time,” it belongs in an automation backlog.

Document:
- trigger
- inputs
- desired output
- biggest exception
- human approval point

## Short post 5
The first automation should usually be boring.

Lead routing.
Report preparation.
Form-to-CRM cleanup.
Follow-up drafting.
Internal request triage.

Boring + repeatable + measurable is often a better starting point than “AI everything.”
